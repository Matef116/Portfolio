Plan International / DAPP: Ready for Tomorrow (YIEP)

1000 to train, 891 to hire, 12 months, and no idle qualified candidates in the market. So I built new programmes to make one.

Client / DonorDansk-Arabisk Partnerskabsprogram (DAPP), Plan International Egypt
LocationEgypt
RoleProject Manager
Team11: 1 Coordinator, 3 Operations, 2 Graphic Designers, 2 Marketing, 3 Partnerships
Duration12 months
ApproachMarket-demand survey → programme design → multi-channel concurrent execution
Scale1,000 trained + 891 hired · ages 18–35 · 50% female

The Mandate

A Youth Inclusion and Employment Project. Ready for Tomorrow is a fully free employment pathway for ambitious young graduates, designed to enhance the employability and economic inclusion of Egyptian graduate youth aged 18 to 35, with a 50% female target. Contracted volume: TVET for 891 beneficiaries and hiring for 891 beneficiaries, against an original design ambition of 1,000 trained at 90% employment conversion.

  • Output Stream 2: Young people, in particular women, have market-responsive technical skills, knowledge of finding employment, and greater access to the labour market
  • O2.1 Technical Skills Development: number of young people benefitting from market-responsive technical skills development, including on-the-job training
  • O2.2 Labour Market Linkage: number of young people matched with employment opportunities
  • O2.3 Employability Linkage: number of young people matched with on-the-job training, apprenticeships and internships
  • O2.4 Private Sector Linkages: number of private sector company partnerships made, including for on-the-job training, market-responsive curriculum development, job and business fairs, and preference for job opportunities

The Challenge

A 90% training-to-employment conversion demand, in a market where the genuinely scarce resource was not vacancies: it was active, unemployed, trainable job seekers who could be trained, matched, and placed into decent work. The programme's constraint was supply, not demand.

The Execution

  • Tripled the training volume against plan, treating the conversion requirement as funnel mathematics rather than an aspiration.
  • Ran a market survey through Wuzzuf and deliberately targeted software, HR, and data analytics roles: the only intersection carrying both high demand and high supply, and therefore the only one that places beneficiaries at scale.
  • Designed Digitera: an AI-centred programme across two tracks: one business track covering every non-software role, and one dedicated AI track.
  • Designed Next Path to capture the entire target audience that Digitera did not fit.
  • Partnered with the Ain Shams University Career Center to train on campus and reach a materially different candidate pool.
  • Ran CX Hiring Day and the Next Summit, and partnered with training institutions to work with their students inside the execution window.
  • Executed every activity stream concurrently under active risk management with close budget surveillance: no overspend permitted.

Results Delivered

  • Training volume tripled to protect the employment conversion requirement.
  • Two new technical programmes designed, launched, and institutionalised: Digitera and Next Path.
  • Ain Shams University Career Center partnership established, opening a new candidate channel.
  • All activity streams executed concurrently with zero budget overrun.
  • Now running its 2026 cycle: an 891-beneficiary target structured into 50 cohorts on a two-week calendar cadence through August 2026.
