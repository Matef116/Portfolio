Training

MCIT: Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI), Rounds 3, 4 and 5

115 cohorts running in parallel. 43 trainers. One dashboard. Over 90% ministry satisfaction.

Client / DonorMCIT, with YAT, EUI, and Career 180 / Freelance Yard
LocationEgypt
RoleTeam Lead
Team2: 1 PM, 1 Operations Specialist
Duration24 weeks per round
ApproachAbsence-resilient risk planning + binding quality rules + weekly performance dashboard
Scale115 parallel groups × 25 beneficiaries

The Mandate

Qualify Egyptian youth to access freelance platforms and diversify their income sources, in cooperation with the Digital Egypt Pioneers campaign: delivered as 115 parallel groups of 25 beneficiaries each, approximately 2,875 learners per cycle, across three consecutive rounds.

The Challenge

Operational complexity at the practical limit: 115 simultaneous cohorts, 43 trainers, two technical providers (YAT and EUI), a consortium partner (Career 180 / Freelance Yard), and a ministry client: every one of them requiring uniform quality and uniform timing across the entire estate.

The Execution

  • Built the risk management plan around the single highest-frequency failure mode: trainer absence. Every session had a named backup, always.
  • Wrote quality management rules binding equally on the operations team, the training coordinators, and the trainers themselves.
  • Instituted timely two-way feedback: from every party, and from beneficiaries against every party.
  • Built a weekly dashboard surfacing the entire machine to me and to executive management, so operational excellence was observed rather than assumed.
  • Held stakeholder management personally across MCIT, YAT, EUI, and Career 180: four organisations, one delivery standard.

Results Delivered

  • 115 parallel cohorts × 25 beneficiaries delivered across rounds 3, 4, and 5.
  • MCIT satisfaction rate above 90%.
  • Training delivery quality above 4.0 / 5.0.
  • Budget held under control across all three rounds.
  • Zero cohort disruption from trainer absence.
