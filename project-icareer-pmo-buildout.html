iCareer / Basharsoft: PMO Build-Out and Portfolio Operating System

Built a project management office, and the team to run it, from zero in three months.

Client / DonorInternal: iCareer, Basharsoft Corporation
LocationEgypt · Jordan · Saudi Arabia
RoleRegional Project Management Team Lead
Team6 Project Managers + coordinators
DurationOngoing: built in 3 months
ApproachPMBOK 7, PMI Talent Triangle v2, tiered governance by PM maturity
Scale12+ concurrent projects · 100M+ EGP · 10M+ SAR

The Mandate

Stand up the project management function for the group's delivery arm (the team, the standards, the governance model, and the reporting spine) and give the C-suite predictive visibility across an entire multi-country portfolio.

The Challenge

A portfolio spanning employment of white and blue collar workers, training for persons with disabilities, youth, students and underprivileged communities, job fairs, summits and events: for private sector CSR clients, donors, INGOs, NGOs and ministries across Egypt and Saudi Arabia, including EU and US-backed funding streams. Projects sharing internal teams while pursuing entirely divergent outcomes. No PMO existed.

The Execution

  • Recruited and built a full team of project managers and coordinators in three months: freshers, juniors, and highly senior PMs governed under one structure.
  • Authored the SOP library covering the full delivery lifecycle, so quality stopped depending on individual memory.
  • Built the cross-functional workflow spine linking operations, marketing, partnerships, project management, and finance.
  • Designed a tiered governance model differentiated by PM maturity: oversight intensity matched to demonstrated capability rather than applied uniformly to everyone.
  • Built an SBI-based feedback logging system and a competency-to-KR mapping framework.
  • Defined the KR set on the metrics that actually move the business: revenue, on-time delivery, DSO, cost variance, and gross profit.
  • Built a weekly status tracker with RAG logic and codified escalation rules: escalation as a rule, not a judgement call.
  • Built a consolidated portfolio budget workbook across 8 projects, normalised to a single currency.
  • Designed a competency-based monthly evaluation tool for Associate PMs, aligned to PMBOK 7 and the PMI Talent Triangle v2.
  • Produced the weekly executive reporting pack (a one-pager per project plus a portfolio snapshot) built for CEO and COO decision-making, not for archive.

Results Delivered

  • PMO built from scratch and fully operational in 3 months.
  • 12+ concurrent projects governed under one standard across three countries.
  • 100M+ EGP and 10M+ SAR portfolio under measured control.
  • Complete expenditure and delivery visibility delivered to the C-suite.
  • Delivery performance made measurable at both individual PM level and portfolio level simultaneously.
