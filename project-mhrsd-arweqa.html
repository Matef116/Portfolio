Employment

MHRSD / Arweqa: Social Security Beneficiaries into Employment

Overspent, zero placements, three months left on the contract. Turned it cash-flow positive, then closed early once the 1,000 target was clearly unreachable.

Delivered for
Client / DonorMinistry of Human Resources and Social Development, Arweqa
LocationKingdom-wide, Saudi Arabia
RoleTeam Lead
Team7: 1 PM, 1 Operations, 1 Data Analyst, 1 Marketing, 1 Partnerships, 2 CX
Duration1 year
ApproachIn-market call centre + database reconstruction + local trainer sourcing
Scale120 of 1,000 target placements verified · 8 cities

The Objective

مشروع التدريب المختص والتأهيل المهني المبتدئ بالتوظيف: Empower social solidarity beneficiaries to find decent jobs matched to their educational background and career preferences. Employ 1,000 beneficiaries in decent jobs, counted only after 100 days from the start date of work. Delivery across Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Mecca, Madinah, Abha, Jizan and Ha'il.

The Challenge

Inherited overspent, with zero placements recorded and three months left to contract end. The ministry's beneficiary database was unreliable and kept changing under us mid-campaign, the target population was extremely hard to reach, the delivery team was operating remotely from outside the Kingdom, and every channel to beneficiaries ran through infrastructure entirely outside our control: the ministry's unified calling line and Arweqa's WhatsApp messaging account, either of which could freeze for weeks with no support while our committed costs kept running.

The Execution

  • Hired a Saudi call centre team and operated through the ministry's dedicated number where it worked: solving reachability and beneficiary trust with the same decision, despite that line staying outside our control.
  • Reconstructed a usable beneficiary database from the ministry's inconsistent, frequently-changing records before spending a further riyal on outreach.
  • Contracted Saudi trainers to deliver employment skills training in-market, in-language, in-culture.
  • Tracked verified placements against burn rate on a weekly cadence rather than waiting for the contract's end date to find out whether the model could work.
  • Once it was clear the communication freezes and database quality would keep verified placements far below the 1,000 required, made the call to close the engagement early instead of running out the clock against a target that wouldn't be hit.

Results Delivered

  • 120 verified placements achieved against the 1,000 target, submitted through Masdar verification, from a starting position of zero.
  • Verified placements were enough to cover incurred costs and keep the engagement cash-flow positive throughout.
  • Delivery executed across 8 target cities Kingdom-wide, remotely.
  • Recognised the 1,000 target was unreachable under the infrastructure the client controlled, and closed the engagement early rather than keep running against a target that wouldn't be hit.
  • Root-cause findings on the database, the communication infrastructure, and the beneficiary population documented for any future re-entry into the market.
