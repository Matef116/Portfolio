Employment

MHRSD / Arweqa: Social Security Beneficiaries into Employment

Overspent, zero placements, four months left on the contract. Closed at a 20% margin.

Client / DonorMinistry of Human Resources and Social Development, Arweqa
LocationKingdom-wide, Saudi Arabia
RoleTeam Lead
Team7: 1 PM, 1 Operations, 1 Data Analyst, 1 Marketing, 1 Partnerships, 2 CX
Duration1 year
ApproachIn-market call centre + database reconstruction + local trainer sourcing
Scale1,000 placements verified at 100 days · 8 cities

The Mandate

مشروع التدريب المختص والتأهيل المهني المبتدئ بالتوظيف: Empower social solidarity beneficiaries to find decent jobs matched to their educational background and career preferences. Employ 1,000 beneficiaries in decent jobs, counted only after 100 days from the start date of work. Delivery across Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Mecca, Madinah, Abha, Jizan and Ha'il.

The Challenge

Inherited overspent, with zero placements recorded and four months left to contract end. The ministry's beneficiary database was unreliable, the target population was extremely hard to reach, and the delivery team was operating remotely from outside the Kingdom.

The Execution

  • Hired a Saudi call centre team and operated through the ministry's dedicated number: solving reachability and beneficiary trust with the same decision.
  • Reconstructed a usable beneficiary database from the ministry's existing data before spending a further riyal on outreach.
  • Contracted Saudi trainers to deliver employment skills training in-market, in-language, in-culture.
  • Rebuilt the cost base around a placement volume that was genuinely achievable in four months: planning to a real capacity, not a contractual wish.

Results Delivered

  • Placement volume achieved that covered all incurred cost and returned a 20% profit margin: from an overspent starting position with zero results.
  • Delivery executed across 8 target cities Kingdom-wide, remotely.
  • 100-day retention condition satisfied on counted placements.
  • Contract closed inside the remaining four-month window.
