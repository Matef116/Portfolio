Employment

UNICEF: Youth Employment at Scale

10,000 placements in a single year. Half delivered before a stop-work order: the machine was already running.

Client / DonorUNICEF, with EYouth and Career 180
LocationEgypt
RoleProject Manager
Team5: 1 Coordinator, 1 Operations, 1 Graphic Designer, 1 Marketing, 2 Partnerships
Duration1 year
ApproachATS & employment-led direct placement, event-layered, coaching-retained
Scale10,000 placements · 140 coaches · ages 18–35

The Mandate

Direct placement of 10,000 beneficiaries aged 18–35 across blue and white collar roles, plus the surrounding capability infrastructure:

  • Employment of 10,000 beneficiaries
  • Deliver 1 job fair
  • Create coach training materials
  • Deliver 4 rounds of training to 140 coaches
  • Deliver support to career centres inside youth centres

The Challenge

A 10,000-placement target against a one-year clock: a volume that no event-led or training-led model can carry on its own. The model had to be built around existing vacancy liquidity, not around programme activity.

The Execution

  • Created Talentex Academy, a pre-recorded e-learning platform designed to give youth the end-to-end skills needed to launch lucrative careers in sales: a curriculum spanning basic market models and the BANT technique through to full sales funnel management and CRM mastery.
  • Anchored delivery on direct placement through Wuzzuf, Forasna, and the Recruitera ATS, where vacancy volume already existed at scale.
  • Layered job fairs and summits on top of the ATS engine for reach and employer density.
  • Delivered pre-hire coaching to every hired candidate, and kept the door deliberately open for them to return for coaching at each new interview attempt: converting one-time beneficiaries into a repeat-engagement pipeline.
  • Built and delivered the coach training curriculum, running four full rounds for 140 coaches.
  • Deployed career centre support into youth centres to extend reach beyond the core team.

Results Delivered

  • Half the annual 10,000-placement target delivered before a donor-issued stop-work order halted the remaining activities.
  • 140 coaches trained across 4 rounds.
  • Coach training materials produced and handed over as a standing asset.
  • Career centre support model deployed into youth centres.
  • A placement engine proven at a rate that would have met the full-year target.
