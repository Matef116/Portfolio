Aramco Gas Stations: Operational Manual to Immersive Training

Never built a VR game before. Shipped one in under 12 weeks at a 75% gross margin.

Client / DonorMohreky, for Saudi Aramco
LocationRiyadh, Saudi Arabia
RoleProject Manager
Team1 Instructional Designer + contracted VR specialist
Duration4 months
ApproachStage-gate production with hardware-in-the-loop testing
Scale10 courses + 1 VR simulation

The Mandate

Convert Aramco's gas station operational manual into 10 self-paced e-learning courses covering onboarding, daily operation, and emergency response, and build a virtual reality training game that simulates the emergency scenarios too dangerous and too expensive to stage in the real world.

The Challenge

Zero prior VR production experience in the house. No VR hardware. A fixed client budget and a hard four-month window. Everything about this project said no.

The Execution

  • Sourced and vetted a freelance VR developer on portfolio evidence rather than promises, then negotiated a rate that protected the project margin before a single line of code was written.
  • Rented VR headsets instead of buying them: converting a capital risk into a controlled, disposable operating cost.
  • Ran stage-gate testing: every build validated on the actual headset, stage by stage, so defects surfaced during production instead of during acceptance.
  • Ran the 10-course e-learning track in parallel with the game build so neither stream ever waited on the other.

Results Delivered

  • VR emergency simulation delivered in under 12 weeks, inside a four-month envelope.
  • 10 operational e-learning courses delivered to standard.
  • 75% gross profit: the highest margin in the portfolio.
  • High client satisfaction, with the game accepted at quality without rework cycles.
