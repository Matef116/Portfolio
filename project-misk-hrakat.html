Misk / Hrakat — Job Shadowing Platform

29,000 learning hours. 3,340 opportunities. 94 organisations. Built by two people.

Client / DonorHrakat — Misk (delivered through Elham)
LocationRiyadh, Saudi Arabia
RoleProduct Owner
Team2 — Full-Stack Developer, UI/UX Designer
DurationSeasonal (April – August), yearly
ApproachAgile, with Microsoft Clarity behavioural instrumentation
ScaleNational high-school programme

The Mandate

Build the digital backbone of a national skill development initiative for Saudi high school students: seamless online registration, interviews and programme progression, automated student distribution to partner organisations, scheduling for field visits and coaching sessions, and admin and coordinator dashboards for full programme control.

View live →

The Challenge

A national-scale programme with a two-person build team, and a matching problem — thousands of students against hundreds of partner organisations — that no manual process could ever carry.

The Execution

  • Held true product ownership: wrote every user story and acceptance criterion, and led the development team rather than routing tickets to it.
  • Automated student distribution to partner organisations, removing the manual bottleneck that would otherwise have capped the entire programme's ceiling.
  • Instrumented the platform with Microsoft Clarity and fed observed friction directly into the backlog — user experience improved on evidence, not opinion.
  • Ran ticketing and FAQ support through Zaytoon, and held the vendor position for Hrakat while liaising with Misk as the programme owner.

Results Delivered

  • 29,000+ learning hours delivered.
  • 3,340 shadowing opportunities created for Saudi high school students.
  • 94 partner organisations and 900+ mentors onboarded.
  • Live platform with full admin and coordinator control, in production.
