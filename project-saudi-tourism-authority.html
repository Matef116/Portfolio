Saudi Tourism Authority: National Trade Partner Knowledge Center

Thirty tracks, twelve contributors, four voice artists: one unified national tourism message.

Client / DonorSaudi Tourism Authority (delivered through Elham)
LocationRiyadh, Saudi Arabia
RoleProject Manager
Team12: 5 Instructional Designers, 1 Graphic Designer, 1 motion graphics agency, 4 Voice Artists, SMEs
Duration1 year
ApproachRolling Wave Planning
Scale5 learning paths × 6 tracks = 30 tracks

The Mandate

Build five learning paths of six tracks each to upskill Saudi Arabia's tourism trade partners (travel agents and tour operators) combining video, photography, FAQs, and marketing content into one unified national message, and a single accessible reference on Saudi destinations.

View live →

The Challenge

Twelve contributors, four vendor categories, and subject matter about live destinations that changes while you are still producing content about it.

The Execution

  • Ran Rolling Wave Planning by design: near-term tracks locked in full detail, later waves deliberately coarse, so destination changes were absorbed instead of triggering a re-baseline.
  • Wrote the RFPs, evaluated the vendors, and hired the freelancers directly: sourcing managed as a delivery discipline, not an afterthought.
  • Ran continuous risk assessment and re-sequenced workflows against shifting client priorities rather than defending an obsolete plan.
  • Delivered weekly progress reports that kept every stakeholder operating on one version of the truth.

Results Delivered

  • 5 learning paths / 30 tracks built for the national tourism trade knowledge center.
  • Unified, current destination reference delivered to travel agents and tour operators nationwide.
  • Tourism messaging standardised and streamlined across the partner ecosystem.
  • Recognition and reward mechanism embedded to sustain learner engagement beyond launch.
