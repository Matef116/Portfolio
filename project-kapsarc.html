E-Learning

KAPSARC: Self-Paced Internal Policies Learning

Nine courses, three tracks, two voices, one motion graphics agency: delivered on time at a 65% gross margin.

Client / DonorKing Abdullah Petroleum Studies and Research Center (KAPSARC)
LocationRiyadh, Saudi Arabia
RoleProject Manager
Team4: 1 Instructional Designer, 2 Voice Artists + motion graphics agency, Full Stack Developer
Duration3 months
ApproachParallel production pipelines against an international standards checklist
Scale3 tracks × 3 courses

The Mandate

Transform KAPSARC's existing content into self-paced training programmes monitored through the organisation's internal LMS (SAP SuccessFactors), meeting international e-learning standards across every asset class: text, images, interactions, audio clips, sound effects, and illustrations.

The Challenge

International quality standards, a three-month window, and a production chain spanning an internal designer, an external motion graphics agency, and two voice artists, plus an integration between Elham's native LMS and SAP SuccessFactors, and an SSO integration that forbids anyone but an internal employee from accessing the LMS. Every link in that chain was a potential single point of failure.

The Execution

  • Architected the output as 3 tracks × 3 courses so production ran as three parallel pipelines rather than one serial queue.
  • Contracted the motion graphics agency against a defined quality bar and fixed deliverable, not an open hourly arrangement.
  • Cast one male and one female voice artist to give each track a distinct tonal identity while keeping the recording schedule elastic enough to absorb retakes.
  • Held every asset against the LMS compliance and international standards checklist before it was permitted into final assembly.
  • Executed SSO integration with SAP SuccessFactors, and built an auto-transfer from the course page on SAP SuccessFactors to the Elham LMS without the user feeling they had left the original LMS.

Results Delivered

  • 9 courses across 3 tracks delivered on time and in full.
  • International e-learning standards met across all six asset classes.
  • 65% gross profit.
  • High-quality production accepted for deployment on KAPSARC's internal LMS.
  • SAP SuccessFactors integration and SSO enablement delivered.
