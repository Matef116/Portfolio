Coca-Cola HBC — Youth Empowered

101% of target. 160+ webinars. 100,000+ youth reached. Gross margin held above 75%.

Client / DonorCoca-Cola Hellenic Bottling Company
LocationEgypt
RoleTeam Lead
Team5 — 1 PM, 1 Operations, 1 Graphic Designer, 1 Marketing, 1 Partnerships
Duration6 months
ApproachExpert-led mass webinar model with rigorous attendance de-duplication
Scale30,000 trained · 500,000 digital reach · ages 18–30

The Mandate

YouthEmpowered is a flagship programme by Coca-Cola Hellenic Bottling Company, designed to give youth the opportunity to bridge the gap between market expectations and educational standards. Train at least 30,000 youth through digital platforms, universities, NGOs, influencers, and job seeker databases, and reach 500,000 digitally across multiple platforms.

The Challenge

Enormous mobilisation volume against a six-month clock — and a hidden measurement trap: youth were so engaged that they attended multiple cohorts and sessions, inflating raw attendance while the unique attendee target went unmet. Solving the wrong number would have failed the contract while looking like success.

The Execution

  • Chose a scale-first delivery model: two-hour flagship webinars leaning deliberately on the brand names of expert speakers and the pull of the topics themselves to attract audience volume rather than paying for reach.
  • Managed attendance de-duplication as a first-class discipline — the contracted metric was unique attendees, so the reporting architecture had to defend that definition rigorously.
  • Ran outreach across digital platforms, universities, NGOs, influencers, and job seeker databases in parallel rather than sequentially.

Results Delivered

  • 101% of the unique attendee target achieved inside the six-month duration.
  • 160+ webinars conducted.
  • 100,000+ youth reached across Egypt.
  • 40% repeat engagement rate.
  • Gross profit maintained above 75%.
