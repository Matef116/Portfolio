Life Makers Foundation × UNHCR: Refugee Job Fair

Three weeks to deliver. Social media prohibited. 110% attendance and a 60% match rate on the day.

Client / DonorLife Makers Foundation, UNHCR
LocationEgypt
RoleProject Manager
Team2: 1 Operations Specialist, 1 Partnerships Officer
Duration3 weeks
ApproachOwned-database mobilisation via SMS and direct calling
Scale350+ target attendees · ages 16–40

The Mandate

Facilitate matching between 350+ Sudanese job seekers and blue-collar employers. Attendance goal: 350+. Demographics: 80% Sudanese refugees, 20% Egyptians. Job type: blue-collar. Age range: 16 to 40.

The Challenge

Three weeks to mobilise an entire job fair, and a strict prohibition on using social media for outreach, removing the default channel for event mobilisation entirely, for beneficiary protection reasons that could not be negotiated.

The Execution

  • Fell back immediately to owned assets: our own beneficiary database plus Life Makers' established refugee network.
  • Ran a targeted SMS campaign backed by direct calling: high-intent, high-trust channels that work without any public exposure of the beneficiaries.
  • Recruited employer supply in parallel with attendee mobilisation, so vacancy density matched attendee volume on the day rather than lagging it.

Results Delivered

  • 110% of the attendance target achieved.
  • 7 companies present with 400+ open vacancies.
  • 60% on-the-day matching rate.
  • 1,400+ beneficiaries reached through the event.
  • Delivered in 3 weeks with a team of two.
