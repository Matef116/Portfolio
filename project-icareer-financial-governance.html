iCareer: Financial Governance System

Budget approvals were dying in inboxes across 13 projects. I replaced them with a state machine and a unique ID.

Client / DonorInternal: iCareer, Basharsoft Corporation
LocationEgypt
RoleProject Manager: sole delivery
TeamSole delivery, serving 30 staff across 5 functions
Duration1 month build · 3 months to full adoption
ApproachRoot cause analysis, control accounts, scripted approval state machine
Scale13 concurrent projects

The Mandate

Replace an email-based budget request and approval process with a controlled, auditable financial governance system that gives executive management real-time visibility of every pound committed across the entire portfolio.

The Challenge

Six project managers, seven operations specialists, seven marketing specialists, six partnerships officers and four finance team members were requesting, approving, and processing budget across 13 projects entirely by email. Payments were lost in threads. Tracking was near impossible. Nobody could say which project carried which cost without asking finance directly. Root cause: no single system of record, no cost coding, and no state machine governing approvals.

The Execution

  • Built a budget template with control accounts and cost line items covering the complete chart of expenditure.
  • Added a time-phased budget sheet owned by the project manager, so spend had a planned shape before it had an actual.
  • Built the request front door as a Google Form writing into a scripted sheet: the requester selects the project and the specific cost line item, so coding happens at source.
  • Encoded the approval state machine: PM approves in-sheet, finance processes in-sheet, and every state transition fires an automated notification.
  • Assigned a unique tracking ID to every request, making each one individually auditable.
  • Built the full notification chain: confirmation to requester, alert to PM, approval or rejection notice back to requester, handoff to finance on acceptance, and proof of payment returned to both PM and requester on completion.
  • Built the live dashboard: expenditure per project, per cost line item, per month, queryable at any moment by anyone.

Results Delivered

  • 13-project portfolio spend made visible in real time to executive management.
  • Full adoption across all five functions within three months of launch.
  • Lost payments and untraceable costs eliminated as a category of problem.
  • Executives self-serve their own reporting: no finance request required.
  • System remains in active daily use and under continuous iteration.
