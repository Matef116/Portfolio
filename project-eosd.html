EOSD — Employment Ready Programme for Persons with Disabilities

Target was 60. Trained 87. 95% client satisfaction, 4.5 out of 5 delivery quality.

Client / DonorEOSD
LocationEgypt
RoleProject Manager
Team5 — 1 Coordinator, 1 Operations, 1 Graphic Designer, 1 Marketing, 2 Partnerships
Duration6 months · 3–4 cohorts
ApproachDeliberate multi-network sourcing + individual coaching layer
Scale60 target / 87 delivered · ages 18–35

The Mandate

Foster employment readiness for 60 Persons with Disabilities in Egypt by developing their soft and digital skills across three to four training cohorts of 15 attendees each, over six months. Disability types in scope: sensory (visual and hearing impairment) and loco-motor.

The Challenge

Reaching a dispersed, hard-to-source target population inside six months, while delivering training that is genuinely accessible across three distinct disability types — not accessible on paper, accessible in the room.

The Execution

  • Built the sourcing network deliberately across charities, NGOs, universities, and Persons with Disabilities communities rather than relying on open advertising that this population does not see.
  • Layered individual coaching on top of group training, so readiness was built person by person.
  • Held delivery to an explicit quality bar with beneficiary feedback measured per cohort, not just at close.

Results Delivered

  • 87 persons trained against a target of 60 — 145% of target.
  • 3 beneficiaries placed into employment by programme close.
  • Delivery quality: 4.5 / 5.0.
  • Client satisfaction: 95%.
