Employment

CFYE: Challenge Fund for Youth Employment

Halved a 50-point gender gap by designing three programmes that were never in the contract.

Client / DonorChallenge Fund for Youth Employment (CFYE)
LocationEgypt
RoleTeam Lead
Team9: 1 PM, 1 Coordinator, 2 Operations, 2 Marketing, 2 Partnerships, 1 Graphic Designer
Duration2 years
ApproachFunnel-stage intervention design + Calendly-based coaching automation
Scale20,000 trained · 11,900 matched · ages 18–35

The Mandate

Through developed technology and networks, increase accessibility of decent employment for Egypt's youth (especially women) enhancing their career-specific skills and knowledge. Five contracted outcomes, two years, an ambitious target:

  • O1: 100 new jobs created for youth at iCareer
  • O2: 11,900 jobs matched for youth, 60% female target
  • OP1: 8 new releases shipped to the VCC software
  • OP2: 1,000 new active partners or employers
  • OP3: 20,000 job seekers trained or coached, 80% female target

The Challenge

Taken over at the half-year mark with the female participation target running roughly 50 points short: in a labour market where the highest-volume vacancies were blue-collar roles with structurally low female supply. The gap was not going to close by doing more of the same.

The Execution

  • Diagnosed the gap as a funnel problem, not a marketing problem: the programme was chasing female registrations when it needed female hires.
  • Designed and launched three new initiatives inside the existing grant envelope: She Gets Hired, She Leads, and the Fempower Summit.
  • She Gets Hired targeted only women who already held a scheduled interview: applying coaching at the single highest-conversion point in the entire funnel, working exclusively on candidates close to hire.
  • Engineered the operational spine in Calendly: candidates self-select from available coaches and book directly, the system verifies eligibility automatically, issues email and calendar invitations to both parties, and handles rescheduling and follow-up end to end: an initiative that scaled significantly without adding a single head.
  • Channelled placement volume through Wuzzuf, Forasna, the Recruitera ATS, and 1,000+ direct employer partnerships.

Results Delivered

  • Female participation gap cut from ~50% to 25%: halved through a major pivot and net-new programme design.
  • Employment target achieved despite a blue-collar-dominant vacancy mix.
  • 1,000+ employer partnerships activated.
  • Three new programme lines created and institutionalised inside the original grant, at no additional cost to the donor.
  • A fully automated coaching booking system that grew the initiative without growing the team.
